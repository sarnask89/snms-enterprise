## 2025-05-15 - Polish Localization and Accessibility
**Learning:** The CRM portal primarily uses Polish for its interface. Accessibility features like ARIA labels must be consistently localized in Polish to ensure a seamless experience for screen reader users.
**Action:** Use Polish ARIA labels (e.g., 'Szukaj urządzeń', 'Opcje urządzenia') that match the surrounding UI text.
