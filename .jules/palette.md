## 2025-05-14 - Polish ARIA labels for icon-only buttons
**Learning:** The application UI is primarily localized in Polish, and icon-only interactive elements require context-aware ARIA labels in Polish to meet accessibility standards and maintain a consistent language experience for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always use Polish translations (e.g., 'Edytuj subskrypcję') for `aria-label` attributes on icon-only buttons in the crm-portal-ts frontend.
