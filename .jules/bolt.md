## 2025-05-26 - Optimized N+1 and Redundant Table Fetches in Customer Devices
**Learning:** The `customer_devices` router followed an anti-pattern of fetching the entire `Customer` table into a dictionary for manual ID-to-Object mapping in Python. This resulted in O(N) database overhead where N is the total number of customers, regardless of the number of devices being displayed. Additionally, the templates triggered N+1 queries for `tariff` names through the `Subscription` model.
**Action:** Use SQLAlchemy `joinedload` for many-to-one (customer) and one-to-many (subscriptions.tariff) relationships. Remove redundant full-table fetches and rely on the pre-loaded relationship attributes. Always check for manual mapping dictionaries in routers as they often hide performance bottlenecks.
