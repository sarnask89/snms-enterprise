
## 2026-07-07 - [Modernization of Subscriptions Page]
**Learning:** Transitioning from legacy Heroicons to Lucide icons and using semantic color tokens (success, warning, error, neutral) provides a more consistent and accessible experience. Adding Polish ARIA labels and toast notifications significantly improves the micro-UX for CRUD operations.
**Action:** When modernizing Nuxt UI v4 pages, always prioritize semantic color tokens over raw colors and ensure icon-only buttons have descriptive ARIA labels in the target language.
